## 1. Critical Bugs to Fix

### A. Hardcoded Scattering Coefficients Overriding Uniforms
- Bug: In shaders/atmosphere.gdshader (around line 447), beta_r and beta_m are hardcoded:
  ```glsl
  vec3 beta_r = vec3(0.0035, 0.0085, 0.021);
  float beta_m = 0.002;
  ```
  This completely ignores and overrides the exported uniforms rayleigh_coeff and mie_coeff passed from planet.gd.
- Fix: Wire rayleigh_coeff and mie_coeff directly into the scattering calculations, ensuring inspector and UI adjustments take immediate effect.

### B. Severe Cloud Undersampling Due to Full-Atmosphere Raymarch
- Bug: The raymarch marches linearly/power-law across the entire atmosphere span t_start to t_end (which spans 40–200+ units from space), while clouds only exist within a narrow band of ~3.5 units (r_c_bot to r_c_top). With only 16–48 steps, 0 to 2 steps hit the cloud volume, resulting in severe slicing, stepping noise, missing cloud banks, or paper-thin appearance at glancing angles.
- Fix: Implement dedicated segment intersection for the cloud deck:
  - Compute analytical ray-sphere intersections for both the atmosphere shell [t_atmo_start, t_atmo_end] AND the cloud shell [t_cloud_start, t_cloud_end] (r_c_bot to r_c_top).
  - March the cloud layer within its dedicated interval using uniform or jittered steps calibrated to cloud thickness, while computing atmosphere in-scattering efficiently without wasting cloud samples on empty space.

### C. Color Extinction & Alpha Blending Washout
- Bug: In atmosphere.gdshader, lines 630–638 calculate:
  ```glsl
  float avg_trans = clamp((atmo_transmittance.r + atmo_transmittance.g + atmo_transmittance.b) / 3.0, 0.0, 1.0);
  float final_alpha = clamp(1.0 - avg_trans, 0.0, 1.0);
  ALBEDO = total_color / max(final_alpha, 0.001);
  ALPHA = final_alpha;
  ```
  Rayleigh scattering attenuates blue wavelengths much faster than red. Averaging atmo_transmittance into a scalar alpha destroys chromatic extinction on the underlying terrain and ocean. At sunset, terrain and oceans appear desaturated/grey rather than warm and tinted by twilight atmosphere.
- Fix: Correct the alpha blending model. Use premultiplied alpha (render_mode blend_premul_alpha) or additive/modulative integration so that ALBEDO = total_color and background extinction correctly preserves wavelength-dependent scattering.

### D. Pole Pinching / Singularity Artifacts
- Bug: UV mapping in sample_cloud_density_at uses spherical equirectangular coordinates:
  ```glsl
  float lat = asin(clamp(norm_c.y, -1.0, 1.0));
  float v_uv = 0.5 - lat / 3.141592653589793;
  ```
  This creates visible pinching and distortion spirals at the North and South poles.
- Fix: Apply polar blending (smoothly transition to triplanar or polar-corrected mapping near |y| > 0.85) or use seamless spherical mapping.

### E. Self-Shadowing Stepping & Noise Banding
- Bug: In-cloud light march uses two fixed relative probe positions (0.25 and 0.70), creating harsh step banding and pitch-black cloud undersides instead of realistic volumetric soft shadowing.
- Fix: Implement a 4-tap or 6-tap progressive shadow ray with exponential step spacing towards the sun, incorporating multiple scattering and the Beer-Powder effect.

## 2. Visual & Quality Enhancements

### A. Dual Henyey-Greenstein Cloud Phase Function
Replace the empirical polynomial phase function with a realistic dual-lobe Henyey-Greenstein phase function:
  P(theta) = k * HG(theta, g_forward) + (1 - k) * HG(theta, g_backward)
- Forward lobe (g1 ≈ 0.75 - 0.82) produces dramatic silver linings around the sun.
- Backward lobe (g2 ≈ -0.15 - -0.25) generates the glory / heiligenschein opposite the sun.
- Parameterize lobe blend (k ≈ 0.7 - 0.8).

### B. Multiple Scattering & Beer-Powder Law
Enhance cloud lighting with the Wrenninge / Ebert multiple scattering approximation:
- Compute primary extinction via Beer-Lambert law: T1 = exp(-d * sigma_t).
- Compute secondary scattering octave: T2 = A * exp(-d * sigma_t * B) (e.g. A ≈ 0.5, B ≈ 0.25).
- Combine with the Powder Effect: 1.0 - exp(-2.0 * d * dt) for forward-facing fluffiness that prevents dark silhouettes on illuminated edges.

### C. Atmospheric Sunset & Ozone Chappuis Absorption
- Integrate an Ozone (Chappuis) absorption layer (h ≈ 15 - 30 km) with coefficient beta_o ≈ (0.65, 1.88, 0.085) * 1e-6. This prevents the horizon from turning unnaturally yellow-green during sunset and produces realistic twilight blues.
- Ensure smooth sunset transition across the day/night terminator without sharp dark cutoff lines.

### D. Cloud Density Shaping & 3D Noise Optimization
- Optimize the 8-layer 3D noise evaluation in sample_cloud_density_at:
  - Early-out immediately when macro envelope / coverage is 0.
  - Evaluate coarse low-frequency shapes first (Layers 1–2).
  - Condition micro-detail evaluation (Layers 3+) on distance from camera and distance to cloud surface, avoiding expensive 3D texture samples inside solid cloud cores or at long distances.
- Retain billow and cauliflower sculpting while eliminating graininess via 3D blue-noise or spatial dither with camera-tracking temporal jitter.

### E. Dynamic Ocean & Terrain Cloud Shadows
- Synchronize cloud shadow calculation in shaders/ocean.gdshader so that the shadow projection accounts for cloud altitude and sun angle identically to the volumetric cloud raymarch.
